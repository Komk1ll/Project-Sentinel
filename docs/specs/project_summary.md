# Project Sentinel - Specification Summary

## Concept & World
- **Genre**: Single-player isometric browser game.
- **Theme**: Mutual aid, reconstruction, and restoration of a small Russian town following a malfunction in municipal automation robots.
- **Target Playtime**: 10–15 minutes complete narrative episode.

## Art & Pipeline
- **Original 3D Models**: Blender master models.
- **Rendering**: Orthographic isometric render (Azimuth 45°, Elevation 30°).
- **Runtime Assets**: Isometric 2:1 sprite sheets and texture atlases loaded in Phaser and rendered with Three.js WebGL effects.

## Priority Tiers
- **P0 (Mandatory Contest Release)**:
  - 1 district map, 3 interior locations, 3 mandatory missions.
  - Complete gameplay loop: movement, interaction, inventory, robot repair, saving.
- **P1 (Polish & Quality Improvements)**:
  - Richer environmental clutter, additional robot interaction animations, enhanced audio.
- **P2 (Future Evolution)**:
  - Subsequent episodes and world expansion.
